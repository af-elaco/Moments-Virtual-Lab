Task

This copies our lab kit: a centre-zero metre rule resting on the blue stand at its 0 mark, clamps with hooks, 100 g hooked masses (weight 1 N each) and a 0 to 10 N newtonmeter clamped at 25 cm on the left.

  1. Drag 100 g masses from the tray onto the hook of the clamp on the right.
  2. Drag the clamp along the rule to the distance in the table. Read the distance from the rule.
  3. Drag the newtonmeter ring down (or use Pull harder and Ease off) until the rule is level. The bubble sits between the two lines.
  4. Read the newtonmeter to the nearest 0.05 N and type it in the table.
  5. Repeat for every row, then look at the graph.
